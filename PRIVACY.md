# APK App Store Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 19, 2026**

APK App Store is a self-hosted Android app marketplace used to discover, download, install, purchase, and update APK applications.

## Information the app may process

Depending on which features are enabled, APK App Store may process:

- **Account information** such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier when you sign in.
- **Google sign-in information** provided by Google after you choose to sign in with Google. APK App Store does not receive your Google password.
- **Purchase information** such as the app purchased, amount, selected payment method, transaction/reference ID, payment status, and basic contact details used for manual payment verification.
- **Device and installed-app metadata** such as Android version, package names, installed version codes, and update status. This is used to determine whether an app is installed and whether an update is available.
- **Download and update information** such as requested app/release, checksum verification result, and download status.
- **Technical information** required to operate and secure the service, such as request timestamps, basic server logs, and error information.

APK App Store does **not** ask for or store your banking PIN, Easypaisa/JazzCash PIN, Google password, or card PIN.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- provide account sign-in and profile features;
- restore purchases across devices when account services are enabled;
- show owned and paid apps;
- verify payment submissions;
- detect installed versions and available updates;
- deliver and verify APK downloads;
- prevent abuse and protect the store;
- provide support and maintain the service.

## Permissions

APK App Store may request Android permissions needed for its core functionality, including:

- **Internet access** to load the store catalog, account data, purchases, and APK files;
- **Notifications** to tell you when updates are available or downloaded;
- **Install unknown apps / request package installs** so Android can show its installer for downloaded APKs;
- **Installed-app visibility** to compare package names and version codes for update detection;
- **Network state** to support Wi-Fi-only background update downloads.

Android may still require a final user confirmation before installing or updating a sideloaded APK.

## Storage and service providers

The production backend is designed to use Cloudflare services for API processing, database storage, and file storage. Google identity services may be used when Google sign-in is enabled. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Sharing and sale of data

APK App Store does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers needed to operate the app, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

## Data retention and deletion

Account and purchase records may be retained while an account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, support, payment records, and legal obligations. When account services go live, users will be provided a way to request account deletion or data deletion through the support/account flow.

## Security

The project is designed to use HTTPS, protected authentication/session tokens, role-based admin access, SHA-256 APK verification, and private backend secrets. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should keep their device and account credentials protected.

## Children

APK App Store is not intended for children under 13.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

## Contact

Before public launch, the official support/contact method will be published inside APK App Store and its official distribution page.
